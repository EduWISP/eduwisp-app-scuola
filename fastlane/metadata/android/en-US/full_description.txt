EduWISP School is the app for staff of schools using EduWISP, the free and open-source platform to manage school tablets while protecting minors' privacy.

The menu depends on your role:

• Teacher: your classes, students and the status of their tablets, the allowed apps. Unlock an app for the lesson (30 minutes to 4 hours): it relocks by itself when it expires, even offline.
• App requests: when a student asks for an app from their tablet, you see their reason and the family's opinion, and decide with a reason. If you approve, the app is allowed only for that student.
• Class coordinator: also the classes you coordinate and their requests.
• Head teacher: the school overview, all classes and all requests.

Privacy first:
• you only see what your role allows, as in the school's web portal;
• no student data is stored on the phone;
• notifications only say how many requests are pending, without names;
• no ads, no analytics, no third-party services.

You need your school's EduWISP server address and your credentials. To try it there is a demo with fictitious data only: server https://demo.eduwisp.org, user demo, password demo (read-only).
